# Naming Conventions & Descriptive Variable Policy

### 1. Descriptive and Self-Documenting Identifiers
- **Full, Explicit Names**: Always use clear, descriptive, and self-documenting names for variables, fields, constants, properties, parameters, and function/method arguments.
- **Zero Tolerance for Single-Letter Variables**: NEVER use one-letter identifiers (e.g. `s`, `p`, `u`, `r`, `c`, `e`, `k`, `v`, `i`, `x`, `y`, `d`, `a`, `b`, `t`, `m`, `h`, `q`, `o`, `n`, `l`, `g`, `f`, `w`, `z`) anywhere in the codebase.
- **Strict Prohibition of Shortening & Cryptic Abbreviations**: NEVER shorten or abbreviate variable, field, or parameter names.
